# Mi-Hy-Prosthetic-Rhizosphere
Mi-Hy is a circular Hydroponic-Bioelectrical System for urban agriculture that merges Microbial Fuel Cells with hydroponics. Featuring a bioengineered prosthetic rhizosphere, it eliminates synthetic nitrogen use. By valorizing wastewater and CO2, Mi-Hy bioremediates nutrients, fixes carbon, and generates electricity for sustainable plant growth.

## 🔬 Core System Architecture & Innovations

As the lead for Work Package 2 (WP2), I coordinate interdisciplinary deliverables across an 8-partner international consortium, including KU Leuven, Paris Research, and Sony CSL, to engineer a fully self-sustaining hydroponic ecosystem[cite: 8, 10]. 

### The Prosthetic Rhizosphere
I pioneered the conceptualization and design of a bio-engineered root-zone interface termed the "prosthetic rhizosphere"[cite: 10]. This engineered environment hosts specific electroactive and diazotrophic microbial communities that maximize natural nitrogen fixation, effectively shifting the paradigm of hydroponic nutrition by eliminating dependencies on synthetic fertilizers[cite: 10].

### Catholyte Nutrient Recovery & Optimization
The system architecture directly integrates MFC waste-streams as a complete replacement for inorganic nutrient feeds[cite: 10]. To rigorously validate nutrient pathways and biomass yield, Experiment 3 utilized synthetic catholyte complemented with Sonneveld's solution. Critical natural catholyte water-softening testing was subsequently conducted between Experiments 3 and 4 to ensure structural and chemical stability prior to further scale-up. I continuously evaluate the chemical composition and electrical output of this catholyte to ensure zero loss in crop yield[cite: 10].

### Plastic-Free Smart Ceramic Matrices
To overcome the sustainability bottleneck posed by traditional plastic agricultural components, I led a strategic pivot in collaboration with material scientists at KU Leuven[cite: 8, 10]. We successfully translated complex biological requirements into technical material specifications to design and prototype vitrified "smart" clay pots[cite: 8, 10]. These porous ceramics serve as the structural backbone of the Mi-Hy ecosystem[cite: 10].

### AI-Driven Phenotyping & Light Algorithms
* **Light-Power Optimization:** I authored a bespoke algorithmic approach to light-intensity modulation, balancing real-time plant photosynthetic requirements against the fluctuating power outputs of bio-electrochemical systems[cite: 10]. 
* **Data-Driven Stress Diagnostics:** I collaborate directly with Sony CSL and Paris Research to process complex image datasets[cite: 10]. This AI-driven phenotyping system accurately distinguishes between nutrient deficiency and environmental stress in real-time[cite: 10].

## 🤝 Leadership & Commercialization Frameworks

* **TRL / TPL Development:** I am currently defining Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) and Technology Performance Levels (TPLs) to transition the ceramic-based hydroponic modules into market-ready agricultural products tailored for the urban farming sector[cite: 8, 10].
* **Industrial Synergy:** I successfully navigated the commercial planning required to recruit LettUs Grow as a primary industrial partner for the Royal Commission for the Exhibition of 1851 grant[cite: 8, 10].
* **Interdisciplinary Mentorship:** I provide co-supervision and proactive pastoral care for a diverse cohort of Ph.D., Master's, and undergraduate researchers, heavily emphasizing the integration of TRLs into their workflows to prepare them for the green-tech industry[cite: 8, 9, 10]. During high-intensity periods, such as the EIC 2026 preparations, I proactively monitor team workloads to prevent burnout and mitigate collaboration fatigue[cite: 8].
